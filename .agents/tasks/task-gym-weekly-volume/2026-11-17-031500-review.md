# Weekly volume per muscle and Recovery readiness cards in the Gym tab (v1)

Adds two cards to the Gym tab. "Weekly volume" shows effective sets per muscle for a Monday-start week (done vs planned, target band 10-14, per-session and ratio warnings, a per-exercise breakdown with a muscle override picker). "Recovery" turns the last 7 days of sleep, stress, mood and stalling lifts into ok / watch / consider-deload, and reuses `gdlon` for the deload button. The pure logic (`gvCredit`, `gvLog`, `gvPlan`, `gvStatus`, `gvFlags`, `gvReady`) takes all inputs as arguments and is tested against the real source extracted from `index.html`. The cards are ported to `gymapp.html`, with ES strings, a v105 bump and a 342-line test file. Counting rules match the brief (RIR<=4 or blank counts, warm-ups never, myo fixed 3 / 1.5, compound secondary 0.5). All `tools/*.test.mjs` pass, and the render harness shows no `undefined` or `NaN` in EN or ES for three weeks.

Watch for: (1) the shared muscle mapping was changed, so the existing plan-config "Weekly sets per muscle" card now shows different numbers (likely); (2) the current-week pill and bar color follow planned sets, not done sets (confirmed); (3) fractional set counts are not localized in ES (confirmed); (4) the render path, click handlers and ES regexes have no automated coverage (confirmed).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

Volume is computed in two parallel passes that share one crediting function. Logged sessions are bucketed by the Monday of their date, and each lift's kind (compound or isolation) comes from the workout's own snapshot so later routine edits do not rewrite history. The planned week comes from the live routine with authored (not deload-halved) sets.

To make the delts "covered indirectly" rule work, the muscle mapping was extended. Rows now credit rear delts, shoulder presses map to front delts, a generic `press` fallback maps to chest, and a Spanish-name table is tried first. These edits sit in the mapping the older plan-config card also uses, so that card's numbers move too.

For the current week the pill, bar fill and ratio warnings are driven by the plan when a muscle has planned sets. The "done" figure is shown as text only. Past weeks use logged data.

Recovery scoring is a points system with explicit data minimums (3 sleep days, 2 stress days, 3 mood days). A deload that ended within 7 days downgrades "consider a deload" to "watch".

## Shared muscle mapping edits reach the plan-config card

`muscleOf` is also what `gMuscleSets` calls for the existing plan-config card. This change adds rear delts as a secondary of every row, a `(overhead|shoulder|military|arnold) press` rule ahead of the chest rule, a last-resort `/press/i` chest fallback, and `MRX_ES`, which is tested first on accent-stripped lowercase names. The edits serve the new card (delts coverage, Spanish routines). The side effect is that the old card's rear-delt totals rise by 0.5 per row set and previously unmapped Spanish names now count. The briefing says the old card should not regress. Its `MBAND` thresholds and rendering are untouched, but its inputs changed. The test `plan: planned weekly matches gMuscleSets conventions` compares the new planned pass to `gMuscleSets` after the mapping change, so it would not catch the old card drifting from its pre-change values.

## Current-week status is driven by the plan

```
cur week:  basis[g] = plan[g] if plan has sets else log[g]
           pill, bar color, ratio check  <- basis
           "done / planned" text         <- log, plan
past week: basis[g] = log[g]
```

Because the pill reads `basis`, at the start of the week every planned muscle shows its final-state status ("On target", "Above target, recovery cost") regardless of work done. That matches the plan-oriented use of the card, but the done number is only text, so a user who skips sessions never sees a "Below target" pill. The ratio warnings and the per-session flags get the same treatment: `gvFlags` runs on both log and plan sessions and the results are deduplicated by group and type, so one message hides whether it came from the log or the plan.

## Logged-week bucketing and snapshot use

Kind comes from `w.sn.items[].e.k`, with `gkind` as fallback for old logs. The muscle override (`S.gym.musc`) and the myo method (`S.gym.mx`) are read live, so changing them rewrites past weeks. That is consistent with an "override" feature, but it is not what the snapshot rule does for kind, and nothing tells the user.

## Spanish number formatting

ES translation of headings, pills, labels and footnotes is complete in the harness output. The gap is numbers: `gvN` emits a dot decimal ("0 / 4.5"), while `gvC` converts the Recovery sentences to comma decimals ("6,8 h"). The two cards therefore disagree in ES.

## Recovery readiness scoring

`gvReady` returns `ok` for 0 points, `watch` for 1-2, `deload` for 3+, and `none` when no input category has enough data. A single stalling lift sets `data=true` but adds no point (`st>=2` or `dec==1` is needed), and the reason list still prints "1 lift stalling", so the pill reads "Recovery OK" above a warning bullet.

## Test coverage

`tools/gym-volume.test.mjs` extracts the real functions and consts from `index.html` and `gymapp.html` by name, so reverting the code fails the tests. Covered: mapping (EN, ES, override), RIR boundary 4/5/blank, Monday bucketing including Sunday, warm-up exclusion, myo with and without prime, snapshot kind beating the live routine, status bands, per-session flags, ratio rules, readiness thresholds, and index/gymapp helper identity. Not tested: `gvWeekState`, `gvInputs`, `gvPerf`, card HTML output, the ES `R` regexes and `gvEs`, and the dispatcher actions.
